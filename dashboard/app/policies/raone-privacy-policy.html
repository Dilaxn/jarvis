1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Raone Radio ("we", "our", "the app") handles your information. Raone Radio is a free live radio streaming application that lets you listen to Raone Radio 24/7.

In short: Raone Radio does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app streams audio and fetches now-playing metadata — that's it. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.

3. Data Handling & Safety

Data storage

Raone Radio does not store any user data on our servers. The only data stored locally on your device is your volume preference, which remains on your device and is never transmitted.

Data sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. No personal information is sold, rented, transferred, or disclosed to any third party for any purpose.

Data retention

Since no personal data is collected, there is no data to retain. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored preferences from your device.

Data deletion

No user data exists on our servers to delete. To remove all app data from your device, simply uninstall the app from your device settings.

Data encryption

All network communication between the app and our streaming servers is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit).

4. App Permissions

Raone Radio requests only the minimum permissions necessary for a radio streaming app to function:

Permission Why it's needed
INTERNET Required to stream live radio audio and fetch now-playing song information
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Allows audio playback to continue when the app is in the background or the screen is off
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Android 14+ requirement for background media playback services
WAKE_LOCK Prevents the device from sleeping during active audio playback so the stream is not interrupted

Raone Radio does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive permissions.

5. Third-Party Services

The app connects to the following external services solely for streaming functionality:

  • AzuraCast streaming server — Delivers the live audio stream and provides now-playing song metadata (title, artist, album art). The server processes standard HTTP request data (IP address, user agent) as part of normal network operations. Refer to your network provider's and the hosting provider's privacy policies for details on how network-level data is handled.

The app does not integrate any of the following:

  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Crash reporting tools (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
  • Social media SDKs
  • User tracking or fingerprinting tools

6. Children's Privacy

Raone Radio is a general-audience app suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information from any user, we also do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).

The app does not contain any age-restricted content, in-app purchases, or social features.

7. Security

We take the following measures to protect your experience:

  • All data transmitted between the app and streaming servers uses HTTPS encryption
  • No personal data is collected or stored, eliminating the risk of data breaches
  • The app requests only the minimum permissions necessary for core functionality
  • No third-party tracking or analytics code is included in the app

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

If a future version of the app introduces data collection of any kind, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the changes will be clearly described.
